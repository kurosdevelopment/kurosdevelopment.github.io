FAQ

Before you open a ticket.

Quick answers to the questions most clients ask first.

Do you provide hosting?

No. Hosting is normally handled by the client unless a specific arrangement is agreed separately.

Can you modify an existing bot?

Yes. Existing code is reviewed first so the work can be quoted accurately and safely.

How much does a custom bot cost?

Pricing depends on complexity and scope. Small projects can start around €40, while larger systems are quoted individually.

Do you work only with FiveM and Minecraft?

No. Those are the main specialties, but general gaming and community servers are also supported.

How do I request a quote?

Join the Kuros Development Discord server and use the quote ticket system. Include as much detail as possible about the project.
