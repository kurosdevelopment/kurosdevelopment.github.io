FAQ

Before you open a ticket.

Quick answers to the questions most clients ask first.

Do you provide hosting?

Yes. Kuros Development also provides optional bot hosting. The hosting service includes a 1-week free trial, so you can test it before deciding whether to continue. Hosting is offered separately from bot development.

Can you modify an existing bot?

Yes. Existing code is reviewed first so the work can be quoted accurately and safely.

How are custom projects handled?

Each project is reviewed individually based on its scope, complexity and requirements.

Do you work only with FiveM and Minecraft?

No. Those are the main specialties, but general gaming and community servers are also supported.

Is hosting included with a bot?

No. Bot development and hosting are handled as separate services. You can purchase a bot without hosting, and the free trial applies only to the hosting service.

How do I request a quote?

Join the Kuros Development Discord server and open a quote ticket. Include your server type, approximate size and the features you need.
